
This thesis focuses on the design and implementation of an intelligent financial assistant called FinanceGPT, which uses advanced natural language processing (LLM) models to interact with users. The main goal of the project is to create an invoice management system that allows users to enter queries in natural language and retrieve relevant financial information without the need for manual search. FinanceGPT will be integrated with an invoicing application built on the Ruby on Rails platform, which will allow seamless processing of queries and translation of their content into database operations.

The thesis explores the possibilities of using LLM models such as GPT models from Hugging Face or OpenAI, and assesses their capabilities to process queries related to financial documents. Based on this, a prototype system will be developed that can answer queries, generate summaries of income and expenses, and provide visualizations of financial data in the form of graphs and tables. The result will be an intuitive and efficient tool that will improve invoice management and provide users with clear information about their finances.
