This thesis focuses on the design and implementation of an intelligent financial
assistant called FinanceGPT, which uses advanced natural language processing (LLM)
models to interact with its users. The main goal of the project is to create an
invoice management system that allows users to enter queries in natural language
and retrieve relevant financial information without the need for manual search. FinanceGPT
is integrated with an invoicing application built on the Ruby on Rails platform,
which will allow seamless processing of queries and translation of their content
into database operations.

The thesis explores the possibilities of using LLM models such as GPT or Claude
models, and assesses their capabilities to process queries related to financial documents.
Based on this, a prototype system was developed that can answer queries,
generate summaries of income and expenses, and provide visualizations of financial
data in the form of graphs and tables. The result will be an intuitive tool that
will improve invoice management and provide users with clear information about
their finances.
